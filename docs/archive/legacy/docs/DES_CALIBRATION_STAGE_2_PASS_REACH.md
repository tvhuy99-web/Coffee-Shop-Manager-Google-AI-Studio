# DES calibration stage 2: symmetric pass contact

Baseline: `b155bc872de178eaca0eb52bf6b5936ffd1291cd`, [population evidence](https://github.com/tvhuy99-web/Coffee-Shop-Manager-Google-AI-Studio/actions/runs/45325294014).
1000/1000 completed; pass accuracy 59.6684%; 1844.056 attempted passes/match; 410.811 intercepted, 319.713 misplaced, 0.792 offside; ball in play 77.346 min.

Change: a pass-specific continuous contact search evaluates acceleration, current movement, reaction, turn, fatigue and playable height for the intended receiver and defenders. Arrival contacts are distinguished from genuinely mid-flight interceptions. Remove the legacy random interception after an unsuccessful geometric contact search. Predict and replay the chosen participant's contact path in DES time; optional checkpoint field retains that path. No direct changes to kick accuracy, tactics, shot resolver, timing defaults or law. Contact/control separation, loose-ball recovery, lead passes, and goalkeeper contests are NOT implemented by this stage. Contact prediction is a local approximation, with explicit receiver tie preference until the contest model is introduced; the intended receiver alone is eligible for clean-pass reception. Test real decision/event paths and repeat identical populations. Do not claim realism or release readiness from a successful CI job. Engine version and migration must be reconciled before release.
